Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected by Bedlessbot (the "bot") and the Bedless Nation website at bedless.mester.info (the "website"), how that data is used, and the choices you have. Bedlessbot is operated by Mester for the sole purpose of serving the Bedless Nation Discord server. It is not a public, multi-server bot.

Bedlessbot is fully open source. Anyone may review exactly how data is handled in the public repository at github.com/MesterMan03/Bedlessbot.

1. Who we are

The data controller is the operator of Bedless Nation ("we", "us", "Mester"). You can contact us about any privacy matter through the Bedless Nation Discord server: discord.gg/bedless-nation-691898152277508146.

2. Data we collect and why

We only collect the minimum data needed to run the bot's features. Specifically:

2.1 Leveling and leaderboard

  • Discord user ID and XP total. When you send messages or spend time in voice channels, the bot awards XP and stores your user ID together with your accumulated XP. This powers your level, level roles, the /rank command, and the public leaderboard.
  • We do not store the content of your messages for leveling. The bot only checks that a message is non-empty in order to award XP.

2.2 Roles and applications

  • Granted role IDs. When you are awarded a role through the application system, your user ID and the granted role ID are stored so the role can be restored automatically if you leave and rejoin the server.
  • Application proof references. When you apply for a role using /apply, we store a reference to your proof (a submitted link, or a cryptographic hash of an uploaded file) and your user ID, to prevent duplicate or reused submissions.
  • Infraction counts. If a moderator issues an infraction on an application, we store your user ID and a counter, used to enforce the application rules.

2.3 Birthdays

  • Birthday date (optional). If you choose to set your birthday with the birthday command, we store your user ID and the date you provide so the bot can post birthday wishes. This is entirely optional and only stored if you opt in by running the command.

2.4 Website accounts and comments

  • Discord OAuth login. If you log into the website with Discord, we store your user ID, username, avatar, and OAuth access/refresh tokens so we can keep you signed in and show your profile. Tokens are used only to identify you to Discord and are never shared.
  • Pack comments. If you post a comment on a resource pack, we store your user ID, the comment text, the pack it relates to, and a timestamp.
  • Push notification subscriptions. If you enable push notifications, we store the subscription endpoint and keys needed to deliver them, together with your user ID. You can disable these at any time from the website.

2.5 AI chatbot (opt-in)

  • Bedlessbot includes an optional AI assistant. It only ever reads a message when you explicitly mention or reply to the bot, and the first time you do so you must agree to a consent prompt before anything is processed.
  • When used, your message text (and any image you attach) is sent to OpenAI's API to generate a reply, and a short rolling history of recent messages is held only temporarily in memory to provide conversational context. This history is never written to disk and is cleared when the bot restarts.
  • Message content is used solely to generate a real-time response. It is not used to train any machine learning or AI model. Per OpenAI's API data usage policy, data sent through the API is not used to train OpenAI's models.
  • If your question requires up-to-date information, the assistant may perform a web search through a third-party search provider (Tavily) to find an answer.

3. Third-party services

To provide its features, the bot and website rely on the following third parties:

  • Discord – the platform the bot operates on.
  • OpenAI – processes chatbot messages to generate replies (opt-in feature only).
  • Tavily – web search used by the chatbot when current information is needed.
  • YouTube Data API – used to validate that a submitted application video link is public.
  • hCaptcha – protects website comment submissions from spam and abuse.
  • Matomo (self-hosted) – privacy-focused website analytics, loaded only after you accept analytics cookies via our consent banner.

4. Analytics and cookies

The website uses a self-hosted Matomo analytics instance to understand general usage. Analytics cookies are only set after you give consent through the cookie banner, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Essential cookies (such as the login session cookie) are required for the website to function.

5. How long we keep data

We retain data for as long as it is needed to provide the relevant feature (for example, your XP total is kept while you participate in the server). Temporary chatbot conversation context exists only in memory and is not persisted. You may request deletion of your stored data at any time (see below).

6. How your data is shared

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties except the service providers listed in Section 3, strictly to operate the features described here. Some information you provide is public by nature within the server — for example, your username, avatar, level, and leaderboard position are shown on the public leaderboard.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Opt out of the chatbot: simply do not mention or reply to the bot. Your message content is never sent to the AI unless you address the bot directly after consenting.
  • XP exclusions: server administrators can exclude specific channels and roles from earning XP.
  • Access and deletion: you may request a copy or the deletion of the data we store about you by contacting us through the Discord server. Deleting your data may reset features such as your XP and level.
  • Log out: you can log out of the website at any time, which clears your session cookie.

8. Children's privacy

Bedlessbot is used within Discord and is subject to Discord's minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below the minimum age permitted by Discord's Terms of Service in their country.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

For any questions or requests regarding this policy or your data, contact us through the Bedless Nation Discord server: discord.gg/bedless-nation-691898152277508146.
